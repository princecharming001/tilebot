How it works

1 · See

Finds the 4×4 grid

Looks for the sixteen light tiles in whatever you give it, whether that is a shared window or a screenshot, and reads each letter against a library of tile shapes.

2 · Think

Scores every word

Walks the board for every connected word in an everyday-English dictionary, then ranks by the game's own scoring: tile values times a length bonus.

3 · Play

You swipe, or the Mac app does

In the browser you swipe the words yourself. The Mac app goes one step further and swipes them on the mirrored phone, with a human-looking hand.

Autoplay for Mac

A small menu-bar app. It waits until a fresh board appears in iPhone Mirroring, plays that round to the clock, then goes back to waiting. It never touches a round you started yourself.

Setup, in order

  1. 1
    Unzip and move Tilebot to Applications, then open it.

    It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app. It lives in the menu bar (look for the grid icon at the top right), not the Dock.

  2. 2
    Allow Screen Recording.

    System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording → turn on Tilebot. Without it the app sees a black screen. The app's Setup window has a button that opens this exact pane and shows a green check when it is granted.

  3. 3
    Allow Accessibility.

    System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → turn on Tilebot. This is what lets it swipe. Without it the swipes silently do nothing, which is why the app checks and tells you.

  4. 4
    Start iPhone Mirroring and open the game.

    Phone locked, nearby, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on, same Apple ID on both. The mirroring window needs to stay visible and in front while a round plays: macOS only passes input to it when it is the active window.

  5. 5
    Click Check my setup, then Watch.

    Check my setup grabs the screen and shows the sixteen letters it reads, so you know every permission works before it ever swipes. Watch arms it. Start a round on the phone, lock it, and walk away.

Not available where iPhone Mirroring is not offered (EU and China accounts). The app does nothing unless a board is on screen; Pause in the menu bar stops it at any moment.

FAQ

Does anything leave my computer?

No. The solver runs entirely in your browser: the word list and tile shapes are downloaded once, frames from your screen are processed locally and never uploaded. The Mac app has no server at all.

Does the solver work on Windows or Linux?

Yes, with a screenshot or typed letters, and with screen sharing in Chrome or Edge. The autoplay app is Mac only because it relies on iPhone Mirroring.

It read a letter wrong.

Tap the tile and type the right letter; the list updates. The shape library covers the letters the game uses most; rarer ones (J, Q, Z) may come up as unsure until they have been seen.

Why did the game reject a word the solver suggested?

The game's dictionary is a little narrower than ours. Words marked rare are the likeliest to be refused; leave "show rare words" off when it matters.

Can I use this in a cash or ranked round?

Technically it does not know the difference. Whether you should is between you and the game's terms of service; a bot in a competitive mode is the kind of thing that gets accounts banned.

Why does the Mac app need the mirroring window in front?

iPhone Mirroring only accepts input when it is the active window, and it ignores input sent any other way. That is Apple's design, so while a round plays the Mac is busy. Start a round and step away.
