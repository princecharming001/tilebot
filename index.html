Connected in four steps

  1. 01Download Tilebot on your Mac

    Unzip it, drag it to Applications and open it. It lives in the menu bar. Needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or newer.

  2. 02Turn on iPhone Mirroring

    Open the iPhone Mirroring app on your Mac. Your iPhone needs iOS 18, the same Apple ID, and to be locked and nearby.

  3. 03Allow two permissions

    System Settings, Privacy & Security: turn on Screen Recording (so it can see the board) and Accessibility (so it can swipe). Tilebot's setup window opens both for you.

  4. 04Open Word Link and press Watch

    Open Word Link in Triumph inside the mirroring window, then click Watch in the menu bar. Start a round and Tilebot plays it to the clock.
