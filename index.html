How it works

  1. Spots the sixteen light tiles in a shared window or a screenshot, and reads each letter against a library of real tile shapes.

  2. Walks every connected path for words in an everyday dictionary, then ranks them the way the game does: tile values times a length bonus.

  3. Tap a word to see its path. Or let the Mac app swipe the whole round on the mirrored phone for you.

Setup, in five steps

  1. 01
    Move Tilebot to Applications and open it.

    It opens like any other app. It lives in the menu bar, top right, not the Dock.

  2. 02
    Allow Screen Recording.

    System Settings, Privacy & Security, Screen Recording, turn on Tilebot. The app's setup window has a button that opens this pane and shows a check once it is on.

  3. 03
    Allow Accessibility.

    Same place, under Accessibility. This is what lets it swipe; without it the swipes quietly do nothing, so the app checks for you.

  4. 04
    Start iPhone Mirroring and open the game.

    Phone locked and nearby, same Apple ID on both. Keep the mirroring window in front while a round plays, since macOS only sends input to the active window.

  5. 05
    Check my setup, then Watch.

    Check my setup shows the sixteen letters it reads, so you know everything works before it swipes. Watch arms it. Start a round and step away.

Not available where iPhone Mirroring is not offered (EU and China accounts). Pause in the menu bar stops it at any moment.

Frequently asked questions

Everything worth knowing before your next round.

Does anything leave my computer?

No. The solver runs entirely in your browser. The word list and tile shapes download once, and frames from your screen are processed on your machine and never uploaded. The Mac app has no server at all.

Does the solver work on Windows or Linux?

Yes, with a screenshot or typed letters, and with screen sharing in Chrome or Edge. The autoplay app is Mac only because it relies on iPhone Mirroring.

It read a letter wrong.

Tap the tile and type the right letter, and the list updates. Rarer letters like J, Q and Z may show as unsure until they have been seen.

Why did the game reject a suggested word?

The game's dictionary is a little narrower than ours. Rare words are the likeliest to be refused, so leave "Show rare words" off when it matters.

Can I use it in a cash or ranked round?

It does not know the difference. Whether you should is between you and the game's terms of service, and a bot in a competitive mode is the kind of thing that gets accounts banned.

Why does the mirroring window need to stay in front?

iPhone Mirroring only accepts input while it is the active window. That is Apple's design, so the Mac is busy while a round plays. Start the round and step away.
